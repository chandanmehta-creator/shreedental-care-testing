What should I expect at a first visit?+

Your appointment usually begins with a conversation and clinical assessment. If imaging or further evaluation is appropriate, we will explain why and discuss next steps.

Do you treat children?+

Yes. Child dental care is approached in an age-appropriate, reassuring way, with a focus on prevention and positive experiences.

How do I request an appointment?+

Use our appointment request form or contact the clinic by phone. A request is not a confirmed appointment until the clinic reviews and confirms it.

Can I ask about a specific treatment?+

Absolutely. Choose a service in the appointment form and share a short message. Suitability, risks and expected outcomes vary by patient and require professional assessment.
